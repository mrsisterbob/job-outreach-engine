Each service returns data in a different shape, so I wrote the mapping and cleanup that turns them into one consistent record. When a feed changes a field, I trace it back to the source and correct the mapping. That is the same loop your posting describes: connect the feed, normalize it, confirm it matches.

That work taught me to ask where a number came from before anyone builds a chart on it, which is the discipline data QA and reconciliation depend on.
